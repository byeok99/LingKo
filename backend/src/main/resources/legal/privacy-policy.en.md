## 8. Deletion

When the retention period ends or the purpose is achieved, we delete the data without undue delay.

**Process.** When you request account deletion, we first delete your stored audio objects (including previous versions and delete markers). Only once that succeeds do we delete, in a single transaction, your login sessions, assessment jobs and results, evaluation credits, and account data. If the storage deletion fails, we preserve your data, stop the deletion, and let you retry.

**Method.** Electronic files are erased irrecoverably; paper records are shredded or incinerated.

Shared reference data such as the syllable dataset used as the pronunciation baseline does not relate to any identified individual and is therefore not personal data subject to deletion.

## 10. Security

**Organisational.** An internal management plan, a minimal number of personnel handling personal data, and regular training for them.

**Technical**

- Access control and access logging for systems processing personal data
- Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
- Token-based access control; refresh tokens are stored only as SHA-256 hashes, rotated on each use, with the device session revoked if a previously used token is replayed
- Voice recordings held in a private bucket under a per-user path, uploadable only through short-lived signed URLs constrained by file size and content type
- Validation of uploaded files for size, extension, format, and ownership
- Log masking that excludes authorisation headers, tokens, secrets, and unnecessary identifying data

**Physical.** Systems holding personal data run in cloud providers' data centres and rely on those providers' physical access controls.

**Breach notification.** We notify users and regulators without undue delay as required by applicable law. Where the GDPR applies, we notify the competent supervisory authority **within 72 hours** under Article 33 and inform affected individuals without undue delay where Article 34 requires it.

## 14. Transparency of AI Pronunciation Assessment

Pronunciation scores are produced automatically by a speech recognition and pronunciation assessment engine.

- **Logic**: your recording is compared with the reference pronunciation of the target sentence (a normalised string derived by applying Korean phonological rules), and accuracy is calculated at sentence and syllable level.
- **Process**: choose a sentence and record → upload to private storage → the engine compares and scores → results returned → the original recording is deleted.
- **Method**: we use the pronunciation assessment feature of an external speech service. **We do not use your recordings to train artificial intelligence models.**
- **Status**: scores are study feedback and produce no legal or similarly significant effects, so they are not a decision under Article 22 GDPR or Article 37-2 of the Korean Act.

If you disagree with a result, contact «example: privacy@lingko.kr» for an explanation and human review.
